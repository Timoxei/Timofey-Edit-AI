# Imams on Quran 4:34 — "It's allowed to strike your wife"
### Verified video clips with timestamps · researched 2026-07-27

Every timestamp below was verified against the video's own subtitle track (yt-dlp auto-captions) or, for MEMRI Arabic clips, against MEMRI's published transcript page. Timestamped links jump straight to the quote.

**Context:** Quran 4:34 (Surah An-Nisa) — "...as for those [wives] from whom you fear arrogance — advise them, forsake them in bed, and [finally] strike them (wadribuhunna)..."

---

## 1. Sheikh Yaser Nadeem Al-Wajdi — Muslim Center of New York (Flushing, Queens, NYC)
- **What he says:** Lays out the three steps of 4:34 — "you admonish them… you leave them in beds… the third [step] is you strike them" — and per MEMRI's transcript says wife-beating is permissible in rare circumstances (e.g. disloyalty) and that "the purpose is to hurt the wife's dignity," not her body.
- **Where/when:** Lecture at the Muslim Center of New York, streamed on the mosque's Facebook, **Dec 9, 2018**. MEMRI clip #6946.
- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWoQ2Hn1S_4 (MEMRI TV Videos, uploaded 2019-01-15, 3:32)
- **Key timestamps (verified vs captions):**
  - [0:22–0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWoQ2Hn1S_4&t=22s) — the three steps of 4:34 incl. "…is you strike them"
  - [0:44–0:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWoQ2Hn1S_4&t=44s) — "I have a license to beat…" (describing the husband's claimed license, which he then qualifies)
  - [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWoQ2Hn1S_4&t=180s) — "taking a weapon and beating her…" (what is *not* allowed — the striking must hurt dignity, not body)
- **Secondary source:** MEMRI clip page — https://www.memri.org/tv/new-york-cleric-yaser-wajdi-wife-beating-okay-rare-circumstances-hurt-dignity

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtUQpH4T9x4 (MEMRI TV Videos, uploaded 2021-05-24, 2:40)
- **Key timestamps (verified vs captions):**

  - [1:42–1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtUQpH4T9x4&t=102s) — "he is allowed to hit her with a toothbrush. Miswak."
  - [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtUQpH4T9x4&t=118s) — "You're allowed to hit without having…" (limits: no bruising/pain)
