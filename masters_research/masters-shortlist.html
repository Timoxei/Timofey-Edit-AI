How admission actually works for an SDU degree
Germany — recognition, uni-assist, grade conversion

APS is NOT required for Kazakhstan (verified July 2026 against APS country lists and the German missions in Kazakhstan). Re-check kasachstan.diplo.de in Sept 2026.

  • A 4-year Kazakh bachelor from a state-accredited university gives direct master admission. Verify SDU on anabin (“Suleyman/Sulejman Demirel”); the uni-assist VPD de-risks this entirely.
  • Grade conversion (Bavarian formula): 1 + 3 × (100 − 79.53)/(100 − 50) = 2.23 = “gut” — clears typical 2.5 cutoffs; razor-thin at 2.3 (Bremen); “sehr gut” programs stay a stretch.
  • The real filter is subject ECTS (often 60+ ECTS political science/IR + methods). Prepare a module-description annex mapping SDU courses to those buckets.
  • Applying pre-graduation is normal: conditional admission on the 6-semester transcript; diploma due at enrollment Oct 2027.
  • Fees: €0 in most Länder (semester fee €150–400); Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU €1,500/semester (Konstanz, Tübingen). Visa: blocked account ≈ €12,000/yr or a scholarship letter.
France — Études en France, Mon Master, fees
  • Route = country of residence: Kazakhstan is one of the 73 Études en France countries → apply via Campus France Kazakhstan. Mon Master excludes EEF-country residents — public-university masters go inside the EEF file (7 choices).
  • The deadline is early: EEF master files close ~Dec 1–15, 2026 for Fall 2027 (answers by 30 Apr, confirm by 31 May). Sciences Po & private schools use their own portals (Oct 2026–Jan 2027).
  • Public non-EU “differentiated” fee ≈ €3,879–3,941/yr, but many universities vote exemptions down to the €254 national rate — ask each program; Eiffel/France-Excellence holders are exempt.
  • Eiffel (€1,181/mo): the school nominates you — request nomination in autumn 2026. Also check Abai-Verne (French Embassy Kazakhstan) and MOPGA Master Kazakhstan.
  • Visa financial proof only ~€615/month — far lighter than Germany's blocked account.
Canada — the honest math

Canadian committees judge your LAST TWO YEARS, not the overall average — and that's your weak spot (recent semesters ≈ 2.6–2.8/4.0, B-/B, vs the B+/3.0+ bars at the brand schools). Nothing in Canada is a true "Safe" for you. A WES/IQAS credential evaluation (~CAD 220) often converts a Kazakh 79.5%-on-a-50-pass-scale favorably — get one and lead with it.

  • Where internationals actually get funded: thesis-based MAs at smaller universities (Memorial, Regina, Manitoba, Calgary MSS thesis, Carleton EURUS) fund via TA/RA packages of CAD 12–20k/yr written into the offer. The professional schools do NOT fund international masters students — NPSIA says so outright, Munk/UBC awards don't dent a CAD 90k+ bill. Honest portfolio: Memorial + Regina as funded anchors, UQAM as the grade-fit option, NPSIA + GSPIA as the two worth-it Reaches. Skip Munk, UBC, Dalhousie.
  • The Quebec French route is your hidden lever: the famous French tuition treaty covers French/Belgian citizens only (nothing for you), BUT UdeM's Bourse d'exemption is automatic for all internationals (tiered by grades), and uOttawa cuts fees for French-language enrolment. Your 4 semesters of French (last one 95/A) are ~12 focused months from making this real.
  • Visa: SDS was discontinued Nov 2024 — regular study-permit stream for everyone. Proof of funds: CAD 20,635 living (indexed) + first-year tuition + travel — ~CAD 27k to show for Memorial, ~CAD 42k for NPSIA. Russian citizens face extra scrutiny/longer processing — apply from Kazakhstan immediately upon admission (Feb–Apr 2027).
  • Canada's genuine advantage: 24 h/week off-campus work (~CAD 1,600–1,900/mo at minimum wage — it can nearly cover living, unlike the EU) and a 3-year PGWP after any masters. The economics after arrival are better than Europe; the barrier is the up-front proof of funds and tuition.
  • Deadlines cluster early: uOttawa Dec 1, 2026 (earliest); Memorial/Calgary/McGill/SFU Jan 15–31; Munk Jan 28; Quebec schools + BSIA ~Feb 1. IELTS is NOT waived for a Kazakh English-medium degree (SFU states this explicitly) — the September–November 2026 IELTS sitting covers Canada too.
  • Prerequisite trap: NPSIA and uOttawa GSPIA both require intro micro + macro economics with a decent grade — you'd need to add these (e.g., Athabasca online) before/alongside applying.
Language certificates — what to take and when
  • IELTS Sept–Nov 2026 (valid 2 years). Target 7.0; German programs ask 6.5/C1, French English-taught 6.5, Canada 6.5–7.0. Results take 3–13 days — don't slide past November.
  • Your bachelor is English-medium — many programs accept a medium-of-instruction letter from SDU instead. Get IELTS anyway: Eiffel-track schools and Canada want it, and it strengthens a modest-GPA file.
  • French: DELF B2 (valid forever) in autumn 2026 beats TCF (2-year validity). With your last French course at 95/A, B2 is realistic; C1 by Fall 2027 is a stretch — lead with English-taught programs and use French as a bonus.
  • German: zero today — treat every DSH/German-required program as a non-option for 2027 (kept for completeness, tiered Reach).
